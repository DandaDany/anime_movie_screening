# Taiwan theatrical catalog: Japanese animated films ONLY

This collection tracks **Japanese-produced animated films playing in Taiwan**, not all animated movies released in Taiwan.

## Eligibility gate (check on every discovery run)
1. The film/revival/special theatrical screening must be **a Japanese animation production**. Confirm primarily Japanese animation production and creative production from official studio, production committee, film and distributor materials. Japanese IP, Japanese language, anime-like art, a Japanese investor or Japan as a minor co-production credit **alone** are insufficient.
2. It must have confirmed Taiwan cinema screening dates or current real Taiwan cinema showtimes, per existing source and eligibility policy.
3. Cross-border productions led by non-Japanese studios (e.g. Hollywood films with Japanese equity) are outside scope. When primary production is uncertain, do not add to active: research and seek authoritative production credits first.
4. Keep existing Japanese productions, including 2026-10-23 **煙囪小鎮的普佩2：約定的鐘樓**, produced by Japanese STUDIO4℃.
5. Validate **all** tracked, poster, and discovery catalog titles for origin before each PR, not just new additions. Deleting an ineligible film from all three JSON files preserves its historical record in Git.
